Our Story

An extension of your care team, not another vendor.

We bring together certified medical scribes and secure, AI-assisted tools to take the documentation burden off your shoulders.

Every note we produce is reviewed by a trained professional and delivered straight into your EHR — accurate, compliant, and on time. From solo practices to multi-site groups, we tailor our support to the way your team already works, scaling up or down as your needs change.

What Drives Us

Our mission, vision, and values.

Our Mission
To deliver effortless, accurate healthcare documentation and well-crafted software — freeing clinicians to focus on patients, and product teams to focus on the right build.
Our Vision
A world where administrative load never stands between a clinician and their patient, and where good ideas ship as well-designed, well-built software — supported by people and technology working together.
Our Values
Accuracy, confidentiality, and accountability guide every engagement. We treat your patients’ data — and your trust — with the seriousness they deserve.
